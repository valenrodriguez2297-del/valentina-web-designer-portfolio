About me

I am a Public Relations professional and Executive Assistant with hands-on experience building and maintaining live e-commerce stores for real clients. My work includes product setup with multiple variants, catalogs, pricing, collections, checkout flows, and WhatsApp support.

I also create websites and landing pages with AI-assisted development using Claude Code and GitHub, carefully reviewing the generated output before publishing. My background in advertising gives me a strong eye for visual finishing, while my administrative experience brings organization, precision, and reliable execution to every project.

Based in Ubatuba, São Paulo, I am available for fully remote, full-time work.
